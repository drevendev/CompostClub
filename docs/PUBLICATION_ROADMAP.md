## Media and evidence gates

1. Match every claim to current code, real UI or the exact model record. JSON validity does not certify an idea's usefulness.
2. Keep the video fully English, preserve the approved voice/music, and retain the synthetic-voice disclosure plus composer, source, license and excerpt/duck/fade attribution.
3. Keep source revisions and raw model failures in local records. Share only approved public examples; full backups can contain more information than a screenshot or idea card.
4. Do not publish owner research checkpoints, private briefing material, model weights, runtimes, DSNs, browser profiles or absolute owner paths.
5. The current video's no-scroll evidence covers its captured desktop viewport, not every screen size. Smaller layouts may scroll normally.
6. New public stories, clips, social posts, services or domains require separate owner authorization. A successful publication has a real URL and a readback receipt.
