## Story

Two real projects suggest a new possibility. Scriptorium studies writing; Quillgrain carries a voice. Local Gemma produced Echo Cartography, a proposed map of voice changes across drafts. Keep that possibility with its original sources. Then show two earlier sprouts becoming new ingredients.

The chosen result is unchanged from genuine local run 04. It is an idea, not an implemented writing product or validated commercial opportunity. The recompost example uses a separate saved garden with Lexigraph and ClawPulse; these checkpoints are not merged into a fictional history. There is no staged generation click, artificial spinner, or new inference during filming.

## Production

- Picture: actual React application at 1730 × 776 inside the approved 1920 × 1080 cream-paper floral frame. The composer is closed through its normal control; no product CSS or content is hidden for filming. Setup and import screens are outside selected segments.
- All eight captured checkpoints passed zero document/nested scrolling and no-scroll-event checks. Visible text was checked for Cyrillic characters, followed by visual review. Capture used validated saved gardens; no new inference was staged while filming.
- Voice: the approved local Kokoro-82M af_heart, speed 1.0, en-us. No cloud voice service or cloning.
- Pacing: scene lengths follow actual narration; 240 ms dissolves; only exterior speech silence is trimmed. No spoken content is cut or stretched.
